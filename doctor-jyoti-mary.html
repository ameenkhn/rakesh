Doctor Profile

Dr. Rakesh B. Singh

MBBS, FMGE, DNB-CET and MS (Orthopaedics). Dr. Singh completed advanced training in arthroscopy, joint replacement and sports injury through fellowship work in South Korea and Singapore, along with focused observerships in Mumbai and Coimbatore. His professional profile combines trauma care, arthroscopy, arthroplasty, teaching-hospital experience and a sustained interest in sports medicine.

MBBS MS Orthopaedics DNB-CET Arthroscopy Joint Replacement Sports Injury
2007 MBBS from Sechenov Moscow Medical Academy, followed by FMGE qualification in September 2007.

2014 MS Orthopaedics from Pravara Institute of Medical Sciences with Grade A.
2016-2017 Advanced arthroscopy, joint replacement and sports injury training across South Korea, Singapore, Mumbai and Coimbatore.

Areas of Expertise

Procedure groups and operative exposure across the orthopaedic spectrum

Knee Arthroscopy & Ligament Surgery

  • ACL reconstruction and ACL avulsion fixation
  • Meniscectomy and meniscal repair
  • PCL avulsion fixation and PCL reconstruction
  • Return-to-function planning for sports injury cases

Shoulder Arthroscopy

  • Rotator cuff repair
  • Bankart repair
  • SLAP repair
  • Arthroscopic capsular release and subacromial decompression

Arthroplasty & Reconstruction

  • Total knee replacement and total hip replacement
  • Hip hemiarthroplasty with AMP and bipolar prosthesis, cemented and uncemented
  • High tibial osteotomy with medial open wedge and lateral closed wedge techniques
  • Joint preservation and replacement-oriented surgical planning

Upper Limb Trauma Fixation

  • Proximal humerus fixation with PHILOS plate
  • Shaft humerus fixation with DCP plating and interlock nailing
  • Supracondylar humerus fixation with cross K-wire and plating
  • Radius, ulna and distal radius fixation with plating, nailing and K-wire techniques

Lower Limb Trauma Fixation

  • Intertrochanteric fracture care with DHS, TFN, PFN, DCS plating and prosthetic options
  • Femur fracture fixation with interlock nailing, DCP plating, K nailing and TENS in paediatric cases
  • Tibial plateau, tibial shaft and distal tibia procedures using Butters plate, MIPPO, nailing and external fixation
  • Patella and malleolar fixation with TBW, screws, tubular plate and Rush nail techniques

Emergency, Infection & Trauma Support

  • Emergency orthopaedic stabilization in accident and trauma settings
  • Osteomyelitis management involving tibia, femur and humerus cases
  • Implant-based fixation planning guided by fracture pattern and acuity
  • Elective surgery and emergency procedure coverage within teaching-hospital practice
