Our Expertise

Children's bone and joint problems we treat

Many of these problems are easier to correct when they are checked early, while the child is still growing.

Clubfoot from birth

Feet that turn inward from birth (congenital clubfoot), treated early so the child can stand and walk normally.

Knock knees and bow legs

Knees that bend inward or legs that curve outward, assessed and corrected as the child grows.

In-toeing and out-toeing

Feet or legs that turn inward or outward while walking.

Spine curvature (scoliosis)

A sideways curve of the spine in children, picked up early and monitored or treated.

Sudden limping or pain while walking

A limp or leg pain that starts suddenly needs a prompt check to find the cause.

Repeated fractures and weak bones

Bones that break again and again, or bones weakened by Vitamin D deficiency, investigated and treated.
