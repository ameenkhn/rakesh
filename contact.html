Before You Call

A simple checklist to make the first consultation more useful

Bring your reports

X-rays, MRI films, blood reports, old prescriptions and any discharge summaries help the consultation move faster.

Describe the limitation clearly

Explain what you cannot do now: walk, squat, climb stairs, play, work, sleep or bear weight comfortably.

Share prior treatment history

Let the hospital know if you already tried physiotherapy, injections, medication or were advised surgery elsewhere.

Need the fastest route to booking?

Call the appointment desk directly and keep your reports ready. That will usually lead to the quickest next step.
