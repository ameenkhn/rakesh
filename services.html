Clinical Services

High-value orthopedic services built around faster recovery and dependable support

BSG Hospital focuses on services where coordinated surgical planning, hospital support and rehab continuity make a real difference: sports injury care, arthroscopy, arthroplasty, trauma and fracture management, and critical care-backed recovery.

Sports Injury Management

Diagnosis and treatment for ligament tears, meniscal injuries, shoulder instability, sports trauma and return-to-sport planning.

  • Structured assessment and imaging-based planning
  • Non-operative and operative decision support
  • Recovery pathways aligned with activity goals

Arthroscopy & Joint Preservation

Focused intervention for ACL, PCL, meniscus and shoulder arthroscopy cases where stability and function matter.

  • ACL reconstruction and avulsion fixation
  • Meniscectomy and meniscal repair
  • Rotator cuff, Bankart and SLAP repair work

Arthroplasty / Joint Replacement

Advanced evaluation and operative planning for knee and hip replacement when pain, deformity or degeneration limit quality of life.

  • Total knee replacement
  • Total hip replacement
  • Hip hemiarthroplasty and revision-oriented planning

Fracture & Trauma Surgery

Comprehensive management of accident and trauma cases requiring fixation, stabilization and orthopedic follow-through.

  • Upper limb, lower limb and periarticular fracture care
  • Emergency procedures and trauma stabilization
  • Implant-based fixation planning based on fracture pattern

Advanced Joint Pain Evaluation

For chronic pain, arthritis, sports overload and mechanical symptoms, patients receive a clear path instead of vague reassurance.

  • Clinical diagnosis with targeted next steps
  • Options across medication, rehab, injection or surgery
  • Second opinions for existing surgical advice

Critical Care Supported Orthopedics

Higher-risk trauma and postoperative cases benefit from the hospital’s ability to coordinate orthopedic care with critical monitoring when required.

  • High-acuity trauma support
  • Postoperative monitoring in complex cases
  • Multi-specialty coordination for safer recovery
Recovery Pathway

What patients can expect from the service experience

Treatment quality is not just about the operation. It also depends on the pace of decision-making, the clarity of instructions and the strength of recovery follow-through.

01

Consultation and diagnosis

Symptoms, injury history, imaging and mobility deficits are translated into a usable treatment strategy.

02

Procedure planning

Patients understand why a surgery is being advised, what alternatives exist and what recovery will demand.

03

Hospital care and monitoring

Trauma, joint replacement and higher-risk cases are supported by the hospital environment instead of isolated outpatient handling.

04

Rehab and return to function

Movement quality, pain relief and day-to-day function remain central goals after the intervention.
